EMPLOYEE REFERRAL

How my referral process works

Start here before applying. Keeping the order right helps avoid preventable referral issues.

Important eligibility check

Please contact me before applying. If you have already applied to Accenture or have been referred by someone else within the last 12 months, tell me first, as this may affect referral eligibility.

01

Find a suitable role

Use the official Accenture careers site for your selected country. Choose roles that genuinely match your skills and experience.

02

Send Job ID + CV

Send me up to 3 Job IDs with your current CV in PDF format. Please do not apply yet.

03

Wait for confirmation

If a suitable referral can be submitted, wait for the referral email/instructions before completing your application.

A referral is not a job offer.

It does not guarantee an interview, progression or employment. Recruitment decisions remain with Accenture’s recruiting and hiring teams.

AFTER THE REFERRAL

Workday application guidance

Once you receive the referral invitation, complete the application carefully and consistently.

1

Use one account

Keep one myWorkday account. Avoid duplicates.

2

Keep details consistent

Use the same name, email, phone and CV associated with the referral.

3

Choose referral source

Where asked, select the employee referral source matching your invitation.

4

Complete it manually

Fill in the application carefully. Where offered, continue without AI rather than relying on profile autofill.

5

Review everything

Check personal details, experience, education and answers before submitting.

6

Confirm submission

Keep the “Application Received” confirmation and send me a screenshot if we are tracking your referral together.

FAQ

Questions candidates often ask

Are you an Accenture recruiter?

No. I’m an Accenture employee independently helping professionals explore opportunities and understand the employee referral process.

Should I apply before contacting you?

If you want to request a referral from me, contact me first and do not apply yet. An existing application or recent referral may affect eligibility.

How many roles can I send?

Please send up to three suitable Job IDs together with your updated CV in PDF format.

Does a referral guarantee an interview?

No. A referral does not guarantee an interview, progression or employment.

Why are only selected countries listed?

Hub24Marcin focuses on selected international markets rather than presenting referral support as worldwide.
