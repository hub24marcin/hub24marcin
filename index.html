EMPLOYEE REFERRALHow the Accenture referral process works6 steps • Referral first • Don’t apply yet⌄

If you would like me to submit an Accenture employee referral for you, please follow these steps in order. Most importantly, don’t start an application before the referral is submitted.

Do it slowly. Do it right. 💜

Important eligibility check

Please contact me before applying. If you have already applied to Accenture or have been referred by someone else within the last 12 months, tell me first, as this may affect referral eligibility.

01

Find a suitable role

Use the official Accenture careers site for your selected country and choose roles that genuinely match your skills and experience.

⚠️ DO NOT APPLY. DO NOT START THE APPLICATION.

When you find a suitable role, copy the full job-posting link. You’ll send that link to me in the next step.

02

Message me on LinkedIn

Send me up to 3 Accenture job links together with your updated CV in PDF format.

Already connected? Message me. Not connected yet? Connect first and mention that you’re interested in an Accenture referral.

Message / Connect with me on LinkedIn →
03

I review & submit the referral

I’ll open the roles you’ve sent, review them alongside your CV, explain the next steps and suggest updates if needed.

If everything is suitable and you’re eligible, I’ll submit the internal Accenture employee referral for you.

Please still do not apply yourself at this stage.

04

Wait for your referral invitation

After I submit the referral, wait for the official Accenture referral invitation/email. Processing times can vary.

Do not create a separate application while you’re waiting.

05

Check with me before continuing

Once you receive the referral invitation, message me before completing the application.

I’ll explain the correct next steps and highlight important details to follow in Workday.

Do it slowly. Do it right. 💜

06

Complete your application

Complete the application using the official referral invitation/link and follow the guidance we’ve discussed. Keep your personal details consistent and review everything before submitting.

After submission, send me your “Application Received” confirmation/screenshot so we know the process has been completed correctly.

A referral is not a job offer.

A referral does not guarantee an interview, progression or employment. Recruitment and hiring decisions remain with Accenture’s recruiting and hiring teams.

AFTER THE REFERRALWorkday application guidanceSix important steps after receiving your referral invitation.⌄

Once you receive the referral invitation, complete the application carefully and consistently.

1

Use one account

Keep one myWorkday account. Avoid duplicates.

2

Keep details consistent

Use the same name, email, phone and CV associated with the referral.

3

Choose referral source

Where asked, select the employee referral source matching your invitation.

4

Complete it manually

Fill in the application carefully. Where offered, continue without AI rather than relying on profile autofill.

5

Review everything

Check personal details, experience, education and answers before submitting.

6

Confirm submission

Keep the “Application Received” confirmation and send me a screenshot if we are tracking your referral together.

FAQ

Questions candidates often ask

Are you an Accenture recruiter?

No. I’m an Accenture employee independently helping professionals explore opportunities and understand the employee referral process.

Should I apply before contacting you?

If you want to request a referral from me, contact me first and do not apply yet. An existing application or recent referral may affect eligibility.

How many roles can I send?

Please send up to three suitable Job IDs together with your updated CV in PDF format.

Does a referral guarantee an interview?

No. A referral does not guarantee an interview, progression or employment.

Why are only selected countries listed?

Hub24Marcin focuses on selected international markets rather than presenting referral support as worldwide.
